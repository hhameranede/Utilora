# Accessibility and UX review

Check semantic headings, landmarks, accessible names, form labels, error associations, focus order, visible focus, keyboard operation, contrast, reduced motion, and zoom. Verify that interactive targets are usable on touch screens.

For Arabic, verify document direction and alignment in RTL while preserving correct direction for code, URLs, numbers, and Latin tokens. Review all affected locales for clipping, untranslated labels, and layout expansion.

Keep UTILORA's warm-white/gray/orange design language. Do not replace working interaction patterns with decorative visuals. Report issues with page, viewport, reproduction, impact, and evidence.
